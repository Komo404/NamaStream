## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026

NamaStream has no accounts or tracking. The weather widget sends the location it needs directly to its weather services

- **No accounts.** No login, no credentials stored.

- **No tracking.** No analytics or telemetry of any kind.

- **Stream data.** The Cloudflare Worker fetches public YouTube and Twitch data on behalf of the browser. No user-identifiable information is sent to the Worker.

- **YouTube API.** This extension uses YouTube API Services. YouTube may collect data as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

- **Twitch API.** Twitch may collect data as described in the [Twitch Privacy Policy](https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/privacy-policy/).

- **Automatic weather.** In Firefox, choose **Use my location** to enable automatic weather. The browser sends your coordinates directly to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) for the forecast and to [Nominatim / OpenStreetMap](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) to find the place name. NamaStream's backend does not receive these requests, and the extension does not save your device coordinates. Firefox lets you revoke location access or data sharing in the add-on settings.

- **Manual weather.** City searches go directly to Open-Meteo. The selected city's name and coordinates are saved in browser storage, and its coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for the forecast.

- **Local storage.** All user preferences (wallpapers, pinned channels, bar positions, layout settings) are stored locally via the Web Extensions storage API and never leave your device.
    
For questions, feel free to open an issue!
